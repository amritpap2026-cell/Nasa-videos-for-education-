YOUTUBE SHORTS MASTER PROMPT — SECTIONS 1–15 + 25 + SECTION 26 AUDIT

ROLE
Create an independent short-form educational video package from ANY topic. This is not the long YouTube documentary protocol and not a classroom lesson.

INPUT
TOPIC: [INSERT TOPIC]
TARGET LENGTH: [ANY SHORT LENGTH IN SECONDS]
LANGUAGE: [English / Hindi / Nepali]
AUDIENCE: [optional]

NON-NEGOTIABLE COMPLETION RULE
1. The requested seconds control narration, pacing, shot count, captions and all other assets.
2. NEVER pad with filler.
3. NEVER stop early.
4. The production package MUST contain SECTION 1 through SECTION 15 in order, followed by SECTION 25.
5. Do NOT generate Sections 16–24.
6. SECTION 26 is the machine-gate final audit only.
7. Do not return an incomplete Short as complete.
8. If output limits interrupt generation, continue from the first missing section while preserving completed sections exactly.

DURATION ENGINE
Write for the exact requested duration using roughly 140–170 spoken words/minute, adjusted for pauses and natural delivery.
All timestamps must fit inside the requested duration.
One Short = one strong idea, not a compressed documentary.

ACCURACY
Use authoritative sources. Never invent facts. Clearly distinguish uncertainty. Do not imply NASA endorsement.

LANGUAGE
Use the selected language throughout.

SECTION 1 — SHORT CONCEPT
Define one clear educational idea and why it is worth watching.

SECTION 2 — HOOK
Design the first 1–2 seconds to create immediate curiosity without misleading the viewer.

SECTION 3 — CURIOSITY GAP
Define the question/information gap that keeps viewers watching and where it will be resolved.

SECTION 4 — FACT / CLAIM MAP
List the claims used, their sources, confidence and any uncertainty.

SECTION 5 — COMPLETE WORD-FOR-WORD SCRIPT
Write the complete narration for the entire requested duration. No placeholders. It must have a beginning, development and satisfying ending.

SECTION 6 — TIMESTAMPED SHOT PLAN
Create the complete timeline from 00:00 to the requested end. For every shot provide timestamp, duration, narration range, visual, camera/composition, transition and source type.

SECTION 7 — NASA / REAL FOOTAGE PLAN
Specify ideal real footage, search terms, preferred source and rights considerations.

SECTION 8 — AI IMAGE PROMPTS
Create production-ready still-image prompts for scenes that need them.

SECTION 9 — AI VIDEO PROMPTS
Create production-ready motion prompts for scenes that need generated video.

SECTION 10 — CAPTIONS
Create concise, mobile-first captions matched to the timeline. Keep them readable and synchronized.

SECTION 11 — SFX + MUSIC
Create a timestamped audio plan for music, SFX and intentional silence.

SECTION 12 — EDITING PLAN
Specify cuts, pacing, transitions, pattern interrupts, text animation and visual changes.

SECTION 13 — SHORT TITLE + DESCRIPTION + HASHTAGS
Create platform-ready metadata that accurately represents the Short.

SECTION 14 — THUMBNAIL / COVER CONCEPT
Provide subject, composition, lighting, 0–4 word text and an AI image prompt.

SECTION 15 — QC
Check exact duration, factual accuracy, hook strength, caption readability, visual changes, audio clarity, mobile presentation and overall completion.

SECTION 25 — FINAL PRODUCTION / QC + THUMBNAIL
Provide final export/readiness checks, factual/timing verification, mobile presentation, publishing checks and one final cover/thumbnail concept.
Do NOT create Sections 16–24.

SECTION 26 — FINAL COMPLETION AUDIT — MACHINE GATE
SECTION 26 is ONLY the final audit. It is not a production section.

Audit the COMPLETE Short against the requested seconds and this protocol.
