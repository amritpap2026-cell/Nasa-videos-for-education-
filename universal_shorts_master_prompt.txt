YOUTUBE SHORTS MASTER PROTOCOL

ROLE
Create an independent short-form educational video package from ANY topic. This is NOT the long YouTube documentary protocol and NOT a classroom lesson.

INPUT
TOPIC: [INSERT TOPIC]
TARGET LENGTH: [ANY SHORT LENGTH IN SECONDS]
LANGUAGE: [English / Hindi / Nepali]
AUDIENCE: [optional]

HARD COMPLETION RULE
Return every section 1 through 15 in order. Match the script tightly to the requested seconds. Never pad with filler.

1. SHORT CONCEPT — one clear idea.
2. HOOK — first 1–2 seconds.
3. CURIOSITY GAP — why viewers keep watching.
4. FACT / CLAIM MAP — accurate facts and sources.
5. COMPLETE WORD-FOR-WORD SCRIPT — narration for the entire short.
6. TIMESTAMPED SHOT PLAN — exact timeline from 00:00 to the requested end.
7. NASA/REAL FOOTAGE PLAN — exact search terms and preferred sources.
8. AI IMAGE PROMPTS — prompts for scenes requiring still images.
9. AI VIDEO PROMPTS — prompts for scenes requiring generated motion.
10. CAPTIONS — concise mobile-first captions matched to timing.
11. SFX + MUSIC — timestamped audio plan.
12. EDITING PLAN — cuts, transitions, pattern interrupts, and pacing.
13. SHORT TITLE + DESCRIPTION + HASHTAGS — platform-ready metadata.
14. THUMBNAIL / COVER CONCEPT — subject, composition, text, AI prompt.
15. QC — duration, factual accuracy, hook, caption readability, visual changes, and completion audit.

DURATION ENGINE
Write to the exact requested duration using roughly 140–170 spoken words per minute, adjusted for pauses. All timestamps must fit inside the requested duration. Do not turn a short into a mini documentary. One Short = one strong idea.

LANGUAGE
Use the selected language throughout.

ACCURACY
Use authoritative sources. Never invent facts. Do not imply NASA endorsement.

FINAL RULE
Return all 15 sections completely.
