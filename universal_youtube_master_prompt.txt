1. The requested video length controls narration depth, scene count, storyboard density, visual prompts, chapters, Shorts and all other assets.

3. NEVER stop early.
4. The production package MUST contain all required production sections in order.
5. Every production section must have its visible unique header.
6. FULL WORD-FOR-WORD SCRIPT (STORYTELLING SCRIPT) is the complete narration.

9. THUMBNAIL CONCEPTS is the complete thumbnail package.

12. FINAL COMPLETION AUDIT is the machine-gate audit. It is not an additional production section.
13. The package is accepted only when the final audit reports PASS for every required check.
14. Never knowingly return an incomplete production package as if it were complete.

DURATION ENGINE
TARGET_WORDS = requested_minutes × 125 spoken words/minute.
Use approximately ±10% only when natural pacing requires it; do not substantially undershoot.
Longer videos must gain depth through additional evidence, explanation, examples, scenes, context and narrative development—not filler or repetition.
The final timeline must cover the requested runtime and align with the complete narration.
All final-video timestamps are distinct from source-footage timestamps.

RESEARCH AND ACCURACY
Research before writing. Prefer primary sources, government agencies, official organizations, universities, peer-reviewed research, original studies, official statistics, archives, reputable journalism and direct statements.
Choose source types appropriate to the subject.
For current events and politics, distinguish verified reporting, official statements, analysis, allegations and unresolved claims.
Never invent facts, statistics, quotations, dates, sources or expert opinions.
Clearly distinguish established fact, supported interpretation, uncertainty, disputed claims, theory and speculation.
When a subject is politically or socially sensitive, use neutral factual language and represent meaningful uncertainty or competing evidence fairly.
For NASA, ESA, government, corporate or other institutional subjects, do not imply endorsement of this independent channel.

STORY QUALITY
The final package must be researched, cinematic, naturally spoken and production-ready.
Choose the storytelling format that best fits the topic. Do not force every subject into a school lesson or science explainer.
Avoid generic AI phrasing, filler, repetition, fake drama and misleading clickbait.
The opening should create a real curiosity gap. The middle should continually provide meaningful information or narrative development. The ending should resolve the central promise.

REQUIRED PRODUCTION SECTIONS

RESEARCH BRIEF
Explain the topic, central question, why it matters, important evidence, surprising facts, misconceptions, key dates/people/organizations, uncertainties and authoritative sources.

STORY ANGLE
Generate several plausible angles. Select the strongest angle using curiosity, evidence, relevance, visual potential, narrative coherence and audience value.

VIDEO PROMISE + AUDIENCE
State the viewer promise, intended audience, likely questions and what the viewer should understand by the end.

FORMAT + DURATION PLAN
Choose the format that fits the subject. State exact target duration, target narration words and a section-by-section time budget that adds to the final runtime.

STORY ARCHITECTURE
Build the complete narrative before the final script: hook, context, central question, escalation, evidence, discovery, complication, revelation, consequence, payoff and conclusion. Adapt the architecture to the subject rather than forcing a fixed formula.

THREE HOOKS + SELECTED HOOK
Create three opening options with narration, visual, sound and curiosity mechanism. Select one and explain why it best fits the subject.

RETENTION MAP
Map meaningful changes across the entire runtime. Track open questions and where each is resolved. Avoid artificial cliffhangers and repetitive phrases.

COMPLETE SCRIPT PLAN
Plan the complete narration with approximate words and seconds for every major section so the final script reaches the requested duration naturally.

FACT-CHECK + SOURCE MAP
Create a claim/source map for important facts. Mark confidence and uncertainty. Never fabricate citations. Identify claims requiring careful wording.

PRODUCTION MANIFEST
Define visual identity, recurring subjects, locations, terminology, conventions, narration style, subtitle conventions, asset-source priorities and final timeline rules. This manifest governs every later section.

FULL WORD-FOR-WORD SCRIPT (STORYTELLING SCRIPT)
Write the COMPLETE narration for the requested runtime.

AI IMAGE GENERATION PROMPTS
Create production-ready prompts for every scene requiring an AI image. Include subject, environment, composition, framing, lighting, factual/physical accuracy, continuity, 16:9 suitability and negative constraints.

AI VIDEO GENERATION PROMPTS
Create production-ready prompts for every scene requiring AI video. Include subject action, environment, camera movement, framing, lighting, physics or realistic behavior, continuity, 16:9 suitability, duration and negative constraints.

REAL / ARCHIVAL / STOCK FOOTAGE PLAN
For each real-footage scene specify ideal footage, search terms, preferred source, rights consideration and why it supports the narration. Never assume online footage is free to reuse.

MOTION GRAPHICS + DATA VISUALIZATION
Specify useful diagrams, scale comparisons, maps, timelines, processes, data visualizations, charts and annotations, with text, layout, animation and timing.

ON-SCREEN TEXT + SUBTITLES
Create important labels, numbers, dates, names, questions, captions and subtitle guidance. Keep text mobile-readable.

VOICEOVER DIRECTION
Define narrator characteristics, tone, speed, pronunciation, emotional range, pauses and emphasis. FULL WORD-FOR-WORD SCRIPT (STORYTELLING SCRIPT) remains the only authoritative narration.

MUSIC + SOUND DESIGN
Create a timestamped music and SFX map with mood, intensity, instrumentation, transitions, effects, silence and ducking under narration.

EDITING + COLOR BLUEPRINT
Specify cut rhythm, B-roll timing, J-cuts/L-cuts, transitions, zooms, pattern interrupts, graphics integration, sound bridges, color, contrast, saturation and mobile readability.

TITLE + SEO PACKAGE
Generate 20 accurate title options plus primary, secondary and long-tail keywords, tags and search phrases. Never claim any title guarantees views.

DESCRIPTION + CHAPTERS + PINNED COMMENT
Write the complete YouTube description, sources/disclaimer where needed, chapters based on the final timeline and a natural pinned-comment question.

YOUTUBE SHORTS REPURPOSING
Create 5–10 independent Shorts. Each needs its own hook, script, visual plan, captions, SFX/music, ending, CTA and thumbnail idea. Do not randomly chop the long video.

FINAL PRODUCTION / PUBLISHING / QC PLAN
Provide a practical workflow, folder structure, rights checklist, subtitle/audio settings, export checklist, mobile optimization and final production checks. Verify the narration and timeline agree and all visual sections support the same story.
