DURATION ENGINE
TARGET_WORDS = requested_minutes × 125 spoken words/minute.
Use approximately ±10% only when natural pacing requires it; do not substantially undershoot.
Longer videos must gain depth through additional evidence, explanation, examples, scenes and narrative development—not filler or repetition.
PART 12 must cover the requested final runtime and align with PART 11.
All final-video timestamps are distinct from source-footage timestamps.

RESEARCH AND ACCURACY
Research before writing. Prefer primary sources, NASA/ESA, universities, government agencies, peer-reviewed research, official archives and reputable journalism.
Never invent facts, statistics, quotations, missions, dates, sources or expert opinions.
Clearly distinguish established fact, supported interpretation, uncertainty, theory and speculation.
For NASA topics, do not imply NASA endorsement of this independent channel.

STORY QUALITY
The final package must be researched, cinematic, educational, naturally spoken and production-ready.
Avoid generic AI phrasing, filler, repetition, fake drama and misleading clickbait.

PART 1 — TOPIC ANALYSIS + RESEARCH BRIEF
