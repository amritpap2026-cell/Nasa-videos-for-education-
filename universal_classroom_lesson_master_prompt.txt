NON-NEGOTIABLE COMPLETION RULE
1. The requested lesson length controls teacher-script depth, examples, activities, discussion, assessment and timing.
2. NEVER shorten a lesson because the topic is simple.
3. NEVER pad with meaningless repetition.
4. The production package MUST contain SECTION 1 through SECTION 18 in order, followed by SECTION 25.
5. Do NOT generate Sections 19–24.
6. SECTION 26 is the machine-gate final audit only.
7. Do not return an incomplete lesson as complete.
8. If output limits interrupt generation, continue from the first missing section while preserving completed sections exactly.

DURATION ENGINE
Teacher narration is approximately 110–125 spoken words/minute, but classroom interaction also consumes time.
Allocate realistic time for explanation, questions, activity, guided practice and assessment.
The total scheduled lesson time must equal the requested lesson length.
SECTION 11 must contain the complete teacher narration/explanation required for the lesson.

ACCURACY
Prefer NASA, ESA, universities, government agencies, textbooks and peer-reviewed sources. Never invent facts, mission results, statistics, quotes or experiments. Clearly label uncertainty.

LANGUAGE
Use the selected language throughout. Keep recognized scientific names and units where appropriate.

SECTION 3 — PRIOR KNOWLEDGE

SECTION 11 — COMPLETE TEACHER SCRIPT

The script must be long enough for the requested teaching time after accounting for interaction.

SECTION 12 — STUDENT ACTIVITY
Provide a classroom-ready activity with objective, materials, steps, time, expected observations and safety notes where relevant.

SECTION 13 — GUIDED PRACTICE
Provide questions/tasks, progression, expected reasoning and teacher prompts.

SECTION 14 — ASSESSMENT
Provide formative checks and final assessment questions appropriate to the class.

SECTION 15 — ANSWER KEY + MISCONCEPTIONS

SECTION 16 — DIFFERENTIATION

SECTION 17 — HOMEWORK / EXTENSION
Create meaningful follow-up work connected to the learning objective.

SECTION 18 — TEACHER QC
Check factual accuracy, duration, materials, safety, objective alignment, activity feasibility, assessment coverage and lesson completeness.
